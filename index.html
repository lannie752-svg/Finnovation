Pixel-game prototype of a beginner investing app. All money is simulated: fictional funds and prices. The name is a placeholder (APP_NAME in the script).

DEMO PATH

  1. GOALS tab: your rank, plus six measurable goals with bronze, silver and gold medals. Sort by priority or closest first.
  2. PORTFOLIO: what you own. Balance, chart, gain or loss in dollars and percent, holdings, activity, suggestions.
  3. MARKET: search companies, pick a goal, see matching funds, buy.
  4. Use the clock bar (PLAY, +1D, +1W, +1M) to move time. Month 4 is a market dip.
  5. BUDGET: see savings, debt, loans. Rearrange them.
  6. LEARN: investment options, strategies and mind traps.
  7. The i button: judges' notes and demo switches.
